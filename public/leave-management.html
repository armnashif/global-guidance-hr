Leave Policies - Global Guidance Education Consultancy

Annual Leave (22 days/year)

  • Accrues at 1.83 days per month
  • Must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance
  • Maximum 10 consecutive days without special approval
  • Unused days carry forward (max 5 days)
  • Peak admission season (Aug-Oct): Limited approvals
  • Can be combined with public holidays

Sick Leave (10 days/year)

  • Medical certificate required for 2+ consecutive days
  • Inform manager before 9 AM on sick day
  • Unused sick days don't carry forward
  • Extended illness: Contact HR for special arrangements

Emergency Leave (5 days/year)

  • For immediate family emergencies only
  • Death of immediate family: 3 days
  • Hospitalization of dependent: 2 days
  • Notify HR within 24 hours with documentation

Unpaid Leave

  • Available after exhausting all paid leave
  • Requires manager and HR approval
  • Maximum 10 days per year
  • Affects monthly salary proportionally
  • Not allowed during peak admission season

Special Leave

  • Maternity: 84 days (12 weeks) paid
  • Paternity: 7 days paid
  • Marriage: 5 days paid (once per employment)
  • Pilgrimage (Hajj): 30 days (once per employment)
  • Study Leave: Up to 10 days/year with approval

Important Notes

  • Peak season: August to October (university applications)
  • All leave requests subject to business needs
  • Handover required for leaves exceeding 3 days
  • Public holidays: As per Sri Lankan government calendar
  • Questions? Contact HR - Thasbiha S.
