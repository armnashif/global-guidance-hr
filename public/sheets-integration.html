How to Use Google Sheets Integration

Option 1: Export to CSV

  • Click "Export to CSV" below
  • Open the downloaded CSV file in Google Sheets
  • Edit the data in Google Sheets as needed

Option 2: Import from Google Sheets

  • Open your Google Sheet with lead data
  • Download as CSV: File → Download → CSV
  • Click "Import CSV File" below and select your CSV
  • Or copy cells from Google Sheets and paste in "Paste from Sheets" tab

CSV Format Required:

Headers: Full Name, Email, Phone, Destination Country, Intake Period, Status, Lead Source, Assigned Counselor, Notes
